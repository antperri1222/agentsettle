How settlement works

1. Buyer authorizes escrow
2. Provider submits a work artifact
3. Buyer reviews and approves or disputes
4. Settlement calculates 0.5% protocol fee
5. Audit events are recorded

The demo records SHA-256 hashes, not cryptographic proof that work is correct. No real funds, wallets or chain transactions are used.
