# AGNT token: hypothesis, not launch plan

## Rule #1
Do not launch or sell a token to fund an unproven MVP. First find a problem where companies pay to use AgentSettle with USDC and existing chains.

## Candidate real token utility
- Independent validators may stake AGNT to provide objective evidence verification.
- Validated services earn fees; economically provable misconduct could cause slashing.
- Certain protocol fees could be automatically converted from USDC into AGNT for operator compensation **only when this is practical and legally reviewed**.
- Governance should not be the only reason the token exists.

## Risks that matter
- Paying operators in AGNT does not itself create net demand; reward emission can create sell pressure.
- Staking without objectively provable violations becomes ineffective or discretionary slashing.
- Liquidity, volatility, MEV, treasury management and regulatory obligations may outweigh benefits.
- Users should continue paying in stablecoins; AGNT may remain invisible in ordinary UX.
- A Base ERC-20 cannot be Base's native gas asset; paymasters can abstract fees, but actual chain gas remains ETH.
- A custom gas chain adds sequencer, bridge, monitoring, economic security, and other major responsibilities.

## Decision gate
Define an externally operated service that cannot be securely or efficiently provided by a centralized SaaS without a token. Validate necessity, fee budgets, validator ROI, legal posture and customer demand before token creation.
